# Phase 1 — Ideation

Goal: from product context to one approved concept, mapped onto shot types the engine
can make. Output: `media-kit/campaigns/<campaign>/concept.md`.

## 1. Product context (read first, ask second)

Collect, from the project's docs/site before asking:

| Item | Example question |
|---|---|
| Product in one sentence | What does it do, for whom? |
| The one promise | What should a viewer remember? |
| Proof / differentiators | Why believe it? (speed, price, guarantee, privacy) |
| Allowed claims | Which claims are legally safe? Which are banned? |
| Audiences | Who buys? Who influences the buyer? |
| Markets / locales | Which countries, languages, seasons matter? |
| Competitors / status quo | What do people do today instead? |
| Channel | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, paid or organic? |
| Budget per video | Rough ceiling (generation is cheap; retries add up) |

Put the confirmed answers at the top of `concept.md`.

## 2. Audiences → shared jokes → formats

For each audience, list:
- **Pains and rituals** they already joke about (the "everyone has been there" moment).
- **Formats** currently working for them: talking animals, POV, fake security cam,
  street interview, game parody, horror trailer, "edit" with a bass drop, before/after,
  "explained by…", rule-breaking montage, ASMR, stitch/duet bait.
- **Trend timing**: seasons, launches, events that make a format timely.

Avoid formats that need real people's likeness, real brands, sensitive groups, or
politically charged animals/topics in the target market.

## 3. Scenarios

Write 5–10, each:

- **Hook** (first 1–2 s, on screen + spoken): the thing that stops the scroll.
- **What happens**: 3–6 beats, physically visible (models render actions, not ideas).
- **Payoff / product moment**: where the product solves it; one line of CTA.
- **Shot types used** (`shot-types.md`) and any **todo** type it needs.
- **Locales**: does it translate, or is it market-specific?

## 4. Score and recommend

Score 1–5 each, show as a table, recommend 1–2:

| Criterion | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Shareability | People tag friends / comment their own story |
| Product fit | The product is the natural answer, not bolted on |
| Production cost | Few shots, no dialogue/lip-sync, reuses kit items |
| Risk (inverted) | No policy, brand, likeness or taste risk |

Also give a rough cost (`lessons.md` §Costs) and the number of paid generations.

## 5. Hand-off

The user picks one. Write `concept.md`: context summary, the scenario, script-level
beats, shot types, locales, the hook variants to test, open questions. Then go to
Phase 2 (media kit) — list what the concept needs from the kit.
